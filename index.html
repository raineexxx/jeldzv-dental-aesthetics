    Good to know

    Quick answers before your visit.

    Do I need an appointment?

    Booking ahead is best so we can set aside enough time for you. You can book online here or call 0999 737 2818.

    How early should I arrive?

    Please come 10 minutes before your time so we can prepare your records.

    What if I need to reschedule?

    Call or email us at least a day before and we will move your appointment.

    How often should I get a cleaning?

    Every six months is the usual advice, or sooner if your dentist recommends it.

    Is whitening safe for my teeth?

    Professional whitening is done under supervision. We check your teeth and gums first to make sure it suits you.
